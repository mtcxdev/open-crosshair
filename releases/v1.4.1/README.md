## 🌟 What's New in v1.4.1

OpenCrosshair v1.4.1 is a focused ergonomics and layout release designed specifically for real-world gaming displays (including standard 1366×768 laptop screens), eliminating visual clutter, expanding tight margins, and introducing an intuitive UI scaling slider.

### 🎚️ 1. Continuous UI & Font Scale Slider (75% – 130%)
* **Replaced Discrete Buttons**: Replaced fixed font-size button chips with a smooth, responsive **UI & Font Scale Slider**.
* **Global Rem-Based Scaling**: Seamlessly scales typography, hitboxes, sliders, paddings, and panel gaps simultaneously without distortion.
* **Instant Reset & Presets**: Includes a quick "Reset (100%)" button, live percentage readout, and direct scale access in the bottom sidebar.

### 📐 2. Spacious Layout & Reclaimed Workspace
* **Reclaimed 120px+ Vertical Space**: Removed bloated marketing hero headers ("YOUR PERSONAL AIM LAB", step numbers "01, 02", and static footer tickers) so the canvas preview and control inspector receive full vertical height.
* **Expanded Control Column**: Inspector width expanded to a comfortable 380px–460px range, preventing cramped sliders or squished color pickers on any resolution.
* **Eliminated Nested Padding Bugs**: Removed forced outer padding overrides, allowing controls and cards to breathe with natural 16px/20px margins.

### 🎮 3. Gamer-Friendly, Card-Structured Settings
* **Clean Card Containers**: Settings are cleanly grouped into elevated cards with subtle borders and clear section separation.
* **Straightforward Language**: Stripped marketing jargon in favor of clear, direct gamer terminology ("Main crosshair lines", "Black border", "T-Shape mode", "Center dot", "Dynamic recoil").
* **Comfortable Hit Targets**: Enlarged color swatches (28px), slider tracks (8px), toggles, and buttons for fast, effortless clicking.

### ☀️ 4. High-Contrast Light Mode
* High-contrast slate borders (`#CBD5E1`) and crisp card backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` on `#F1F5F9`) ensuring every control is razor-sharp in bright environments.
